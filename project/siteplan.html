Logo //// Site Name E-Shop Rwanda Why this name was chosen: The name clearly identifies the website as an online shopping platform operating in Rwanda. It is short, memorable, easy to pronounce, and suitable for branding, marketing, and future expansion of the business. Optional domain idea: eshoprwanda.com Site Purpose The purpose of E-Shop Rwanda is to provide a convenient online shopping platform where customers can: View current prices of products. Browse categories such as groceries, electronics, coffee, fashion, and household essentials. Add products to a shopping cart. Calculate the total order amount automatically. Place orders for fast delivery across Rwanda. Access information about payment options, delivery services, and customer support. The site is designed to bridge the gap between buyers and sellers by making shopping possible anytime and anywhere. Scenarios The following questions represent common needs of the target audience: Scenario 1 How can I check the current price of groceries before placing an order? Scenario 2 Does E-Shop Rwanda offer delivery services outside Kigali, and how long does delivery take? Scenario 3 Can I pay using Mobile Money instead of a bank card? Scenario 4 Where can I contact customer support if I have a problem with my order?
