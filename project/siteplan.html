E-Shop Rwanda

Website Planning Document

Site Name

E-Shop Rwanda

The name clearly identifies the website as an online shopping platform operating in Rwanda. It is short, memorable, easy to pronounce, and suitable for branding, marketing, and future expansion.

Optional domain: eshoprwanda.com

Site Purpose

E-Shop Rwanda provides a convenient online shopping platform where customers can:

  • View current product prices
  • Browse groceries, electronics, coffee, fashion, and household essentials
  • Add products to a shopping cart
  • Calculate total order amounts automatically
  • Place orders for fast delivery across Rwanda
  • Access payment, delivery, and customer support information

Scenarios

  1. How can I check the current price of groceries before placing an order?
  2. Does E-Shop Rwanda offer delivery services outside Kigali, and how long does delivery take?
  3. Can I pay using Mobile Money instead of a bank card?
  4. Where can I contact customer support if I have a problem with my order?

Color Schema

Color Hex Code Usage
Primary Blue #0056D2 Header, buttons, navigation, links
Accent Yellow #F4C542 Highlights, badges, icons, hover effects
Dark Gray #1F2937 Headings, footer, important text
Light Gray #F5F7FA Page and section backgrounds
White #FFFFFF Cards and readable content areas

Typography

Poppins

Used for headings, navigation links, buttons, and promotional text.

Roboto

Used for paragraphs, product descriptions, forms, footer text, and general content.
