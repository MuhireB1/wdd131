Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about shopping, delivery, payments, returns, and customer support at E-Shop Rwanda.

1. How do I place an order on E-Shop Rwanda?

Browse products, add items to your cart, review the cart, and proceed to checkout.

2. Can I shop without creating an account?

Yes. You can browse products and submit an order using the contact and checkout process.

3. Which payment methods are accepted?

We support Mobile Money, debit and credit cards, and cash on delivery in selected locations.

4. Is Mobile Money payment secure?

Yes. Mobile Money transactions are processed through secure payment channels.

5. Do you deliver outside Kigali?

Yes. We provide delivery services to major towns and districts across Rwanda.

6. How long does delivery usually take?

Kigali orders may arrive the same day, while upcountry deliveries typically take one to three business days.

7. How are delivery fees calculated?

Delivery fees vary depending on your location, order size, and quantity of items purchased.

8. Can I change my delivery address after ordering?

Yes. Contact customer support as soon as possible before your order is dispatched.

9. How can I track my order?

You can contact support with your order details to receive a delivery status update.

10. What should I do if an item is damaged?

Report the issue immediately with a description and, if possible, photos of the damaged item.

11. Do you offer refunds or replacements?

Yes. Eligible products may be replaced or refunded according to our return policy.

12. Can I return groceries or food items?

Perishable products can only be returned if they arrive damaged, expired, or incorrect.

13. Are all products shown on the website available?

Products marked “In Stock” are currently available for purchase and delivery.

14. How often are product prices updated?

Prices are reviewed regularly to reflect supplier changes, promotions, and market availability.

15. Do you sell products from local Rwandan suppliers?

Yes. E-Shop Rwanda supports local producers and suppliers whenever possible.

16. Can I buy multiple quantities of the same product?

Yes. Increase the quantity directly in your shopping cart before checkout.

17. Do you offer discounts or promotions?

Yes. Hot deals, seasonal offers, and promotional discounts are displayed on the home and shop pages.

18. How can I contact customer support quickly?

Use the contact form on this page or reach out through our listed support channels for assistance.

19. What information should I include when reporting a problem?

Include your full name, contact information, order reference, and a clear explanation of the issue.

20. How do I receive updates about new products and offers?

Subscribe to our newsletter to receive weekly promotions, product announcements, and delivery updates.
